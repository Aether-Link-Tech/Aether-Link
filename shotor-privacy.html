Shotor AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Shotor AI is a private assistant app for Android made by Aether Nova (formerly Aether Link). It summarises reports, helps with email, and reminds you of meetings. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone, we do not run any server that receives it, and we never sell or share it.

1. Where your data is kept

Everything the app stores (reports and their summaries, cached email, chat history, settings) is saved only on your phone. Aether Nova does not have a server for Shotor AI and never receives your data. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

2. Email

  • Gmail (Sign in with Google). If you connect a Google account, the app asks for permission to read your email (gmail.readonly) and to send email (gmail.send). It uses this only to show your recent inbox in the app, to summarise and sort new messages, and to send replies that you approve, or automatic replies to routine messages if you switch that option on. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your phone. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Other email (work email). If you connect another mailbox with your email address and password, the password is stored encrypted on your phone and is sent only to your own mail server to read and send your email.
  • The app keeps up to 30 days of email on your phone for offline reading and deletes older messages automatically.

Google API data: Shotor AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the email features you see in the app. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, it is not used to train AI models, and no person at Aether Nova reads it.

3. The AI

  • On-phone AI. By default, the AI runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you give it leaves the device.
  • Optional online AI. If you (or your organisation's administrator) add an online AI key in Settings, the text of email messages and your chat questions are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to create summaries, reply suggestions and answers faster. Before sending, the app removes phone numbers, bank and card numbers, email signatures and older quoted messages. Reports, documents and attachments are never sent online; they are always processed on your phone. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. You can turn the online AI off at any time in Settings.

4. Calendar, notifications and files

With your permission, the app reads and adds events in your phone's calendar to show your meetings and remind you of them, and shows notifications. Files you open or share into the app are read on the phone to create summaries. None of this is uploaded by the app.

5. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data on this phone in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything it stored. Disconnecting an email account in Settings removes its saved sign-in from the phone.

6. Children

Shotor AI is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13.
